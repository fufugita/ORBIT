How motion works

Motion means ORBIT is working, or needs you. At rest nothing moves. Every animation is tied to an engine event, has a rate and an end, and collapses to its end state under reduced motion.

  • The UI ticks every 16 ms. Each animation is a pure function of its state and the tick count, so a golden test can render any frame at any tick.
  • An animation redraws only its own cells: the star, the activity words, a card's track row. Text never re-wraps for an animation; the layout glide is the one exception.
  • Two clocks: the session clock stops while ORBIT waits for you; animations run on wall time and keep moving while it waits.
  • Reduced motion (reduced = true in tui.toml) shows every end state at once. The star becomes a still ✦.
  • In 16 colours or under NO_COLOR, colour-only effects (shimmer, fades, flashes) turn off. Glyph motion stays: the star, the comet and the agent arcs still move.

Layouts you build yourself

The screen is a tree of panels. Each panel shows one view: Conversation, Changes, Terminal, Plan, Activity, Context, Review, or an agent. A split puts its children side by side or stacks them, with a share for each. The default is three columns with the conversation in the middle: Changes, Conversation and Terminal. On startup the panels unfold from the conversation outwards.

  • esc starts arranging. v splits the focused panel right and s splits it down; a picker asks what the new panel shows. p changes what the focused panel shows. x closes it, and its share goes to its neighbours.
  • H J K L swap the focused panel with its neighbour, and focus moves with it. < > change its width by 6% of the split, and - + its height; no panel goes below 16%. = evens out every split. b shows or hides the sidebar.
  • [ ] cycle the presets: columns, build, agents and review. Any change saves the layout as yours, the last preset, stored as a tree in tui.toml (here, in this browser).
  • Panel numbers follow reading order, so 1–9 always match the screen. A view can be open in more than one panel. A click focuses a panel or switches the layout.
  • Under 120 columns the tree is kept but one panel shows at a time: the top bar becomes a switcher and panels slide in from the side.
  • When no Conversation panel is big enough to hold an approval, the card floats above the status line. A decision never hides behind your layout.
  • Layouts are UI state only. They need nothing new from the engine.

Building it in ratatui

  • Keep one Anim { start_tick, dur_ticks, curve } type with progress(tick), plus pulse(tick, hz) and flash(tick, start, dur). Store the tick on the state that changed, never on the widget.
  • The star clock is the one stateful animation: keep k and last as the motion spec in docs/tui/PROMPT.md §10.1 already describes.
  • Mark dirty per region (status line, each panel) and redraw only while some animation is unfinished; when none is, stop the 16 ms tick so an idle ORBIT draws nothing.
  • Detect the tier from COLORTERM, TERM and NO_COLOR. Map tokens once per tier; under 16 colours draw pills as reverse video.
  • Golden tests: render with TestBackend at chosen ticks and compare cells and styles, the way this page's gallery renders every motion at any time.
  • The prototype's modules map one to one: core (buffer, glyphs), anim (curves, star clock), comps and panels (widgets), scenario (event reducer), app (layouts, input).

The session on the Prototype tab is scripted: its events are the ones a real engine run would send, at realistic times. Replies to a prompt you type are scripted too.
